Why pre-submission validation

Stop reacting to denials. Start preventing them.

A denied claim means rework, delay, and lost revenue. The DocBen validator checks every claim the moment it's created — before it ever reaches PhilHealth — and tells you exactly what to fix.

Catch denials early

Missing required fields, invalid case-rate codes, and inconsistent clinical data are flagged before submission — not after weeks of waiting.

Actionable results

Every validation returns a 0–100 quality score, the exact rejection reasons, and the PhilHealth DRG classification.

Submit clean, get paid faster

Clean first-pass submissions mean fewer denials and shorter days-in-AR for your hospital or clinic.
